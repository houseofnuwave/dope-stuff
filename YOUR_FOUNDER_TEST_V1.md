# Founder usability test — 10 authentic pieces

**Before starting:** Use a new account with a unique password, keep the working directory private, and never expose the local service to the public internet.

## Single-item exercise (20 minutes maximum)

1. Open Inventory, create an item with 3–8 original images (front, back, sizing/brand label, flaws). Supply correct brand, size, condition, acquisition cost and asking price.
2. Review the seven-point listing readiness checklist. Do not claim an item is new or authentic without verifying those facts yourself.
3. Open Publish studio. Select Depop and Grailed. Copy and inspect drafts. Do NOT mark published until you personally publish on the marketplaces.
4. Once actually listed, mark both as seller-published. Add official listing links if available.
5. Record the sale *only when sold* and enter its actual sale price, marketplace fees and shipping cost.
6. Open Action Center and remove the remaining marketplace listing yourself, then click "I removed it".
7. Verify Sales & Profit reflects the correct total and download a CSV ledger. Confirm the full backup download contains your items and photos.

## Measure actual customer value

Write down the total time spent in FORM and outside, how long the same process took before FORM, any missteps, and which stages you would pay to automate. Testing two deliberately unlisted sample records is okay, but do not describe simulated posts or transactions as actual commerce.

## Stop and report

Do not include private addresses, payment credentials, or sensitive personal information in bug reports. If you see a record belonging to another account, cannot restore your data, or encounter an accidental claim that a listing was synced, stop the pilot and report the exact action sequence.
