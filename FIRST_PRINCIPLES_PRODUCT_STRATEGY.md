# FORM Seller OS — First-principles product decisions

**Purpose:** Help an independent reseller move a real, one-of-a-kind item from acquired → documented → prepared → seller-published → sold → externally delisted, without losing inventory truth.

## Core promises worth charging for

1. **One source of truth:** a single SKU and verified factual record for every item. The user owns their photographs, costs, and notes.
2. **Speed through structure:** generate channel-oriented copy and reduce retyping, but never claim we have published externally where we have not.
3. **Risk reduction:** seller-confirmed channel status, sale logging and obvious manual delist queue. Do not create a false sense of automatic syncing.
4. **Financial clarity:** separate asking price from recorded gross proceeds and net realized profit. Do not mistake inventory value for cash.
5. **Customer sovereignty:** export and now restore their own archive safely; never hold data hostage to a subscription.

## High-leverage 20% for closed pilot

- One exceptional listing editor with original photos and truthful condition selection.
- Assisted publish kit and seller-owned channel checklist.
- Accurate realized profit and urgent delist warnings.
- Private recoverable seller records and usable mobile/desktop design.

Avoid complex AI copywriting, speculative marketplace integrations, team seats, paid subscription rules and self-hosted microservices until five sellers demonstrate repeat usage.

## Pilot measurement (5–10 resellers)

- Time to create a complete item record with 3+ images, compared with their normal workflow.
- Items listed on 2+ channels; number of manual steps skipped.
- Number of seller-reported live listings still awaiting removal 24 hours after a sale.
- Seller confidence in cost, margin and catalog completeness.
- Return usage after 7 and 14 days.
- Willingness to pay at $14.99 and $39.99 AFTER real workflows, not hypothetical survey alone.

## Product-level constraints

- No marketplace credentials, tokens, scraping, automation or browser bot behavior.
- “Listing quality” measures completeness only; it does not verify authenticity, physical condition or marketplace policy compliance.
- Restoration only changes FORM's local data and **does not reflect or modify current remote listings**.
- Never launch external pilot without HTTPS, persistent database and object storage, monitoring, recovery tests, account recovery and legal policies.
- FORM is a working name pending brand and trademark checks.

## What 6, 12 and 36 months should look like if validated

**Six months:** closed pilot converts into a stable paid beta with accurate inventory records and measurable time saved.

**One year:** users' cross-channel workflows become habitual; improve partner-permitted official integrations based on real demand.

**Three years:** deepen retention through accounting exports, vetted integrations, workflow automation with confirmed permissions, and documented service reliability. These are product aspirations, not forecasts.

**Brand separation:** keep FORM as its own resale SaaS, distinct from OF SUN & INK's editorial wellness identity.
